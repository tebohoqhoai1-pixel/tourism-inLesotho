A special place in Lesotho

Maletsunyane Falls is a famous waterfall near Semonkong in Lesotho. The water drops into a deep gorge, creating an impressive view for visitors and photographers.

This student website introduces the destination and shares simple information to help visitors plan a respectful and enjoyable trip.
