About Maletsunyane Falls

A natural landmark in the highlands of Lesotho.

A NATURAL LANDMARK

Where water meets the mountains

Maletsunyane Falls is located near Semonkong in the mountain kingdom of Lesotho. It is known for its high waterfall and the steep gorge surrounding it.

The falls are a popular point of interest for people who enjoy nature, photography and outdoor experiences. Weather and access conditions can change, so visitors should plan ahead.

Location

Near Semonkong, in Lesotho's mountainous interior.

Landscape

High cliffs, a deep gorge and surrounding highland scenery.

Visitor care

Respect local communities, keep the area clean and travel safely.
